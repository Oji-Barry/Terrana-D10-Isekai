# Sprite list for the combat arena

Every creature in the Chapter 15 Bestiary, plus player heritages. Sprites: 8 to 16 bit style, 4 facings (front, back, left, right), bouncing idle only. "M/F" means make a male and a female variant. Named characters (Demon Kings, dungeon bosses) get one sprite each. Size is the token area on the map.
