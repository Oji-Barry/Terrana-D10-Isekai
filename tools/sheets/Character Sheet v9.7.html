Combat Quick Reference
Turn Structure
  1. Upkeep Phase: Regain mana by your Mana dots (see Mana and Magic) and 1 AP, each +1 if you took damage last turn. Pay all persistent effect upkeep costs (they count toward Max Mana Per Turn). Roll for Burning or Bleeding.
  2. Planning Phase: Discuss strategy with allies. Declare intended actions. ST declares enemy actions.
  3. Move or Use Item Phase 1: Move up to available zones, or use a belt item, or do both by spending AP.
  4. Action Phase: Roll your attack against the target's defense roll. If the target wins, the attack misses. If you win, your excess successes carry forward to the damage roll.
  5. Damage Phase: Roll your damage pool (damage Attribute + weapon Rating) and add the excess successes from the attack. Below BDT = no damage. Meet or exceed BDT = 1 Bashing. Meet or exceed LDT = 1 Lethal. Meet or exceed ADT = 1 Aggravated.
  6. Move or Use Item Phase 2: May move remaining zones if any remain, or use a belt item, or both by spending AP.
  7. Ending Phase: Triggered abilities and end-of-turn effects activate.
Action Points
  • Max AP held: 3. Max AP used per turn: 6. You regain 1 AP during Upkeep (2 if you took damage).
  • Giving AP: Allies may give each other AP freely. It costs no action. You can use AP from any mix of allies, but never more than 6 in one turn.
  • Spend 1 / 3 / 6 AP: Add 1 / 2 / 3 dice to any roll.
  • Spend 1 / 3 / 6 AP: Move 1 / 2 / 3 extra zones beyond your base movement.
  • Spend 1 Willpower (once per turn, no action, mark 1 X): add 3 dice before a roll, or reroll up to 3 failed dice after it.
  • Spend 1 AP: Swap to an alt weapon or retrieve a stowed item instead of using your attack action.
Movement
  • Each zone is one square on the battlefield. Your starting position is your starting zone.
  • Dex 1-4: 1 zone per turn across both movement phases.
  • Dex 5-6: 2 zones per turn.
  • Dex 7: 3 zones per turn.
  • Flight: Minimum 2 zones per turn. Doubles available movement zones. Taking damage while airborne requires a Dexterity save or you begin to fall.
  • Mechanical flight: You must use all of your movement every turn or begin to fall.
  • Standing from prone counts as moving 1 zone.
Items & Equipment
  • Max 3 weapons total. Max 2 wielded at once.
  • Swapping to an alt weapon costs 1 AP or your attack action for that turn.
  • Belt (3 slots, set before combat): 1 item free per turn. 2nd item costs 1 AP. 3rd item costs 2 more AP. Total to use all 3 in one turn: 3 AP.
  • Items may only be used during Move or Use Item phases. Moving and using an item in the same phase requires spending AP.
  • Retrieving a stowed item (not on your belt) costs 1 AP or your attack action.
  • Item Rating (1 to 7) adds to relevant rolls. Rating 6 = A Rank. Rating 7 = S Rank.
Rolls and Successes
  • Roll a pool of d10s. Each die showing 6 or higher is a success. A 10 counts as 2 successes.
  • Attack roll: Attribute + Ability + Combat Style (or Attribute + Magic + school dots for elemental magic) vs. the defender's roll. The defender chooses their own defense.
  • Damage roll: Damage Attribute + weapon Rating (+1 at F up to +7 at S), plus the attack's excess successes. Compare the total to the target's BDT, LDT, and ADT.
  • No critical successes or failures. Bigger margins can give more dramatic results at the ST's discretion.
Mana and Magic
  • Mana Pool = (Magic + Circle Magic + every Magical Knowledge dot) × Mana.
  • Max Mana Per Turn = Fortitude + Mana attribute.
  • Activation cost = rank of effect (1 mana at rank 1 through 7 mana at rank 7).
  • Bonus dice: 1 mana = 1 die, up to your school dots or Max Mana Per Turn. Declare before rolling, for attack or damage. Fire, Earth, Shade: damage only. Air, Bright: attack only. Water, Nature, Battle Aura: either.
  • Instant effects last this turn, no upkeep. Persistent effects cost upkeep each Upkeep Phase equal to their mana cost minus 1. Drop them on your turn at no cost.
  • Recovery per Upkeep: Mana 1-3: 1. 4-6: 2. 7-8: 3. 9: 4. 10: 5. +1 if you took damage. Out of combat, a few minutes of rest refills the pool.
  • Running dry: Spending your pool to 0 drops you unconscious (Incapacitated) until you recover mana.
Ability Specialties
  • A specialty is a specific condition that adds 1 die when the roll fits. Example: Awareness (Scent). Up to 3 per ability.
  • One free at creation for an ability at 4+ dots. Others cost 5 XP with ST approval.
Social Combat
  • Willpower is the health bar. Attacker rolls a Social Attribute + Ability; defender rolls Willpower + Composure. Excess successes deal Willpower damage. Your scales may add bonus or penalty dice.
  • Ends when either side fills their row with X's, or 5+ damage is dealt in one retort.
  • Critical Comeback: A defender who rolls 5+ successes over the attacker ends it in their favor.
Health and Damage Thresholds
  • BDT = Fortitude + Armor rating. LDT = BDT x 2. ADT = BDT x 3.
  • Health penalties stack across all damage types. A full column is not Incapacitated; another level of damage in a full column is.
  • Aggravated damage leaves lasting injuries determined by the ST.
  • Burning / Bleeding: each Upkeep, roll Fortitude + Endurance (+ Resilience if you have it) vs. the effect's rating: success 1 Bashing, failure 1 Lethal.
