Introduction

Backgrounds are the circumstances of your character's life rather than anything inside them. Attributes describe your body and mind, and Abilities describe what you have learned. Backgrounds describe what surrounds you: the people who know you, the resources you can draw on, the reputation you carry, and the networks you move through. They are not you, but they shape what you can do in the world in ways raw ability cannot.

Spending Points: At character creation you have 7 points to spend on Backgrounds, plus any points left over from Merits and Flaws. Freebie Points can also buy Backgrounds at 1 point per dot. Backgrounds are rated up to 7 dots, but no Background can be filled above 5 at creation.

Resources: Every character starts with 1 free dot of Resources, the basic financial footing any working person in Terrana has. More dots are bought normally.

After Creation: Backgrounds cannot be bought with XP. The Storyteller may award Background dots for what you achieve in play: relationships built, resources won, reputations earned.

On Your Sheet: Fill Backgrounds from the top of the Merits, Flaws, and Backgrounds section downward, so they stay apart from your Merits and Flaws, which fill from the bottom up. Guild Standing is recorded under Guilds and Memberships on your OurSpace page.

The list below covers the most common circumstances. If your concept needs a resource or relationship not on it, work one out with your Storyteller. Some Backgrounds, such as Hacker and Subscribers, belong to the modern era and may not exist in games set in another era.

Background List

Like nearly everything in the game, Backgrounds are rated from 1 to 7 dots, and 7 dots is truly enormous. No Background can be filled above 5 at character creation, and 5 is already very high by most measures. Dots 6 and 7 are only reached through play.

Backgrounds

BackgroundDescription
AlliesReliable people who will actively help you when asked. Define each ally's field or affiliation at creation. Each dot is either a new ally or a more powerful one. 1 dot is a capable friend; 3 dots is someone with real power or several solid friends; 5 dots is a person of major influence who will go to bat for you. 7 dots is one of the most powerful people in the world in your corner. Allies have their own lives and limits. They are not servants.
ContactsPeople who feed you information in a chosen field. They will tell you things, but they will not take risks for you. Each dot is a broader network or a deeper source. 1 dot is a single useful source; 3 dots covers a city's worth of whispers in your field; 5 dots means little in your field happens without you hearing of it. 7 dots is an intelligence network that rivals a kingdom's.
FameHow widely you are known, and for what. 1 dot is local recognition in one community; 3 dots is a name known across a continent in your field; 5 dots is a name that opens conversations in places you have never been. 7 dots is a name known in every corner of Terrana, a living legend.
FixerSomeone who works outside normal channels and makes things happen: finding work, solving problems quietly, and connecting people. 1 dot is a local fixer for small jobs; 3 dots can find serious work and make big problems vanish within a region; 5 dots has reach across continents. 7 dots can arrange almost anything, anywhere, for the right price. A fixer has their own interests and their own prices.
FollowersPeople personally loyal to you or your cause, not hired staff. They take risks that Contacts will not, but they are not trained soldiers or specialists. 1 dot is a handful of devoted friends; 3 dots is a small movement; 5 dots is a following large enough to fill a town square. 7 dots is a movement that spans nations.
HackerYour skill at getting into and moving through the CCN and the crystal-based systems that run the modern world. Hacking has existed in any real form for only twenty to thirty years. 1 dot is basic access and navigation; 3 dots gets you through most secured systems with time and patience; 5 dots reaches systems their builders believe are unreachable. 7 dots treats the CCN itself as an open book. Every use needs tools and time, and the Storyteller decides what is possible.
InfluencePull within one institution, organization, or power structure, defined at creation. 1 dot opens doors that would otherwise stay closed; 3 dots gets real favors done; 5 dots means the institution moves when you push it. 7 dots means you effectively control it.
MentorA more experienced person invested in your growth. Each dot reflects both their power and how committed they are to you. 1 dot is a capable teacher with limited reach; 3 dots is a respected master; 5 dots is someone the whole world pays attention to. 7 dots is one of the Seven Strongest, or someone of that standing.
NobilityYou were born into a noble family. 1 dot is minor local nobility with a small estate and a name that matters in one town; 3 dots is an established house with regional reach; 5 dots is high nobility with continental recognition and political weight. 7 dots is a literal monarch or head of state, such as a king or president.
ResourcesWealth, income, and lifestyle. Every character starts with 1 free dot. 1 dot is a modest life: a one-bedroom apartment or a dorm room and the basics covered; 3 dots is wealthy by most standards; 5 dots is generational wealth or a major business. 7 dots is wealth on the scale of a Corporate Kingdom. Monthly income, starting funds, and the cost of living are in Chapter 14; track the money you have on hand next to Resources on your sheet.
RetainersStaff, employees, or trained assistants in your service. Unlike Followers they are professionals: they do the job well, but their loyalty is to the job. Each dot is more retainers or more capable ones. 1 dot is a single assistant; 3 dots is a small skilled staff; 5 dots is a household or company of experts. 7 dots is an organization of hundreds working for you.
Safe HouseSecure places to hide, rest, or lie low. Dots can make one location better or, if you say so at creation, add more locations. 1 dot is a single clean, hidden spot with basic supplies; 3 dots is well equipped for a long stay with some defenses; 5 dots is a fortified, fully supplied base for long-term concealment. 7 dots is a hidden stronghold no one has ever found.
Street CredRespect within a community or scene you define with the Storyteller. It does not have to be criminal: deep roots in a neighborhood or real standing in an underground scene both count. 1 dot is local respect; 3 dots carries weight across a city or region; 5 dots means people who have never met you know what you stand for. 7 dots makes you a legend of your scene worldwide.
SubscribersAn engaged audience that follows you on the CCN, as a creator, public figure, or someone whose life simply caught people's attention. 1 dot is a modest local following; 3 dots is an audience across several regions; 5 dots is reach large enough to move public opinion. 7 dots is one of the biggest names on the CCN. Fame is general recognition; Subscribers is an audience that follows your output. You can have either without the other.

Guild Standing

Guilds are how the modern world organizes itself, and in Monsera they are the government: every citizen 18 or older may join one, and membership is how a citizen gains a political voice. Seats in the parliamentary cabinet are divided among the ten cabinet guilds. Guilds also operate outposts well beyond Monsera.

Guild Standing measures your rank and pull within one guild. Take it separately for each guild you belong to, and record each one with its dots under Guilds and Memberships on your OurSpace page. Being a member at all is free and needs no dots; Guild Standing is what makes the guild work for you.

Limits: Guild Standing is rated from 1 to 7. The Storyteller sets how many dots can be bought in each guild at creation; in most games the limit is 3 dots in any one guild. Higher standing is earned in play.

The Hero Rangers Corps: The HRC is not a cabinet guild, but it works like one for this Background. Your Guild Standing in the HRC is your rank in the Corps, from Rank 1 trainee to Rank 7 Titan candidate (Chapter 3).

Ranks of Standing

DotsStanding
1Member in good standing. You can use the guild's halls, job boards, and services, and other members treat you as one of their own.
2Respected. You are known within your local chapter, trusted with better work, and can call on fellow members for small favors.
3Officer. You hold a recognized post in your chapter, can vouch for others, and have a say in local guild business.
4Chapter head. You lead a local chapter or outpost and can direct its members and resources.
5Regional leadership. You help run the guild across a region or continent.
6High council. You sit on the guild's highest council and shape its direction worldwide.
7Guild head. You lead the guild and hold its seat in Monsera's cabinet.
