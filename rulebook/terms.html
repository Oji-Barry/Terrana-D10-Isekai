Introduction

Every term below is explained in full in the chapter named after it. This index is for quick lookup at the table: terms are grouped by topic and listed alphabetically within each group. Page numbers will be added once the whole book is compiled.

Dice and Rolls

Bonus Die: An extra die added to a roll by a specialty, a Boon, a scale position, AP, Willpower, mana, or an ability.

Called Shot (Ch. 12): Aiming at a specific limb, weapon, or vital spot costs -2 dice. Its effect only happens if the attack hits.

Dice Pool: The number of d10s rolled for an action, usually an Attribute + an Ability, plus any Style or school dots and bonus dice.

Excess Successes: Successes the attacker rolls beyond the defender's roll. They carry forward and are added to the damage roll.

Penalty Die: A die removed from a roll by a Bane, a scale position, a wound penalty, or an enemy's technique. Penalties on enemies stay flat; they never scale with dots.

Success: Any die showing 6 or higher is 1 success. A 10 counts as 2 successes. There are no critical failures.

Character Creation

Affinity, Standard, Aversion (Ch. 6): The three attribute columns. The number next to each is both its cap and its points to spend: 6, 5, and 4. They also set Combat Style or Magical Knowledge dots at creation: 7, 5, and 3.

Attribute Flaw (Ch. 8): One attribute group a heritage can never take as its Affinity column. Hyumans and Vampires have none.

Driving Factor (Ch. 7): What your character wants. Each one belongs to a scale and a side; you start with one for each of your two highs.

Freebie Points (Ch. 6): 7 points to spend at the end of creation (a Newb), or 14 (Veteran) or 28 (Legend) if the Storyteller allows. Attribute 3, Ability 2, Combat Style, Magical Knowledge, or Heritage magic 4, Background 1, Mana 7 per dot. They cannot buy Merits, Flaws, Willpower, Fate, Sanity, or other Heritage traits.

Funds (Ch. 14): The money your character has on hand, tracked next to Resources on the sheet.

Golden Rule (Ch. 6): Nothing on the sheet can be filled above 5 dots at character creation, not even with Freebie Points.

Heritage (Ch. 8): Your character's people. Sets lifespan, an Innate Attribute, an Innate Flaw, an Attribute Flaw, Heritage Merits, and Heritage magic.

Level (Ch. 6): The total of your dots in Attributes (including Mana), Abilities, Combat Styles, and Magical Knowledges (including Heritage magic). Merits, Flaws, Backgrounds, Willpower, Fate, Sanity, and the scales do not count.

Past Life Occupation (Ch. 6): What your character did before arriving in Terrana. Mark PL on 3 abilities tied to it (up to 5 with Storyteller approval). Each PL ability gets a free specialty, and raising it costs new dot x2 XP.

Primary (P) (Ch. 6): Every Combat Style and Magical Knowledge taken in creation Steps 10 and 11, plus the free Heritage magic dot, is marked P and costs new dot x5 XP.

Role (Ch. 6): Your battlefield position: a line (Frontline, Midline, Backline) plus one word for how you fight, such as Backline Mage.

Scale Positions (Ch. 7): Each of the seven Virtue and Vice scales has five positions: High Virtue (2), Low Virtue (1), Central (0), Low Vice (1), and High Vice (2). The number is how many dice it adds or removes.

Secondary (S) (Ch. 6): Every Combat Style and Magical Knowledge gained after creation Steps 10 and 11 is marked S and costs new dot x6 XP.

Specialty (Ch. 6): A condition written next to an ability that adds a bonus die when it applies. Up to 3 per ability. At creation, one is free for any ability at 4 or more dots and one for each PL ability; the rest cost 5 XP.

Trope (Ch. 7): Your character's personality archetype. Each gives a Boon (+1 die with one ability) and a Bane (-1 die with another).

Character Stats

Dots and Boxes (Ch. 11): Willpower, Fate, and Sanity each have 10 dots and a row of 10 boxes. Checks fill from the left, X's from the right, and nothing resets on rest. A full row of checks gains a dot; a full row of X's loses one. None can go below 1 or above 10.

Fate (Ch. 11): Your karmic weight in the world and your power to bend its story. Never spent; built and lost through influence and Fate contests.

Fate Contest (Ch. 11): Both sides roll their Fate as a dice pool; most successes wins, the defender wins ties. Opposing Fate can be pooled.

Guild Standing (Ch. 10): Your rank in one guild, 1 to 7. The Storyteller sets the limit at creation, usually 3. In the HRC, it is your Corps rank.

Mana (Attribute) (Ch. 11): Rated 1 to 10, the only trait with ten dots. Every character starts with 1. Freebies cost 7 per dot; XP costs new dot x7.

Resources (Ch. 10): Wealth, income, and lifestyle. Every character starts with 1 free dot: a modest life in a one-bedroom apartment or dorm room.

Sanity (Ch. 11): Your hold on reality and your dice pool for resisting the Void. Lost dots bring traumas, from a phobia at 4 to severe trauma at 2. At 1 and at 10 the Storyteller takes control of the character.

Willpower (Ch. 11): Mental and emotional resilience and the health track for social combat. Spend 1 per turn, as no action, to add 3 dice before a roll or reroll up to 3 failed dice after it. Each spend marks an X.

Combat and Movement

Action Points (AP) (Ch. 6): Hold up to 3 AP; use up to 6 in one turn. Regain 1 during Upkeep, or 2 if you took damage. Spend 1, 3, or 6 AP for +1, +2, or +3 dice, or for 1, 2, or 3 extra zones of movement.

Shared AP (Ch. 6): Allies can give AP freely at no action cost, from any mix of allies, but no one uses more than 6 AP in a turn.

Zone: One square on the battlefield. Only one creature or object can occupy a zone; nothing is ever "in your zone" except you.

Movement: Dexterity 1 to 4: 1 zone per turn. Dexterity 5 to 6: 2 zones. Dexterity 7: 3 zones. Standing from Prone counts as moving 1 zone. A diagonal step counts as 1 zone. Boulders, walls, trees, deep water, and other blocking terrain cannot be crossed.

Difficult Terrain: Mud, rubble, shallow water, ice, deep snow, webs, thorns, brush, and the like. You can move into it with your normal movement, but your free movement ends there, and you cannot get out without spending AP. Starting your turn in it works the same way. Every zone you move after that, in it or out of it, is bought with AP: 1, then 2 more, then 3 more (1, 3, and 6 AP in all, the same as buying extra zones). Brush and crates are also half cover.

Initiative (Ch. 6): Dexterity + Perception.

Flight (Ch. 8): Anything that flies moves at double its normal movement. Winged flyers use the Athletics specialty Flight.

Mechanical Flight (Ch. 14): Flight from machines, vehicles, and devices. It must use all of its movement every turn or begin to fall.

Empowered Flight (Ch. 13): Battle Aura Rank 6 flight: 6 mana (or 3 Willpower) to take off, 5 mana upkeep at altitude, 3 to hover. Taking damage that meets your BDT forces a Dexterity + Athletics roll or you begin to fall.

Counter (Ch. 12): When your defense roll beats an attack, a Counter strikes back using that same roll as your attack.

Range (Ch. 14): Melee hits adjacent zones (one farther with Reach). Thrown: up to double your Strength in zones. Bows, guns, and ranged spells: anywhere in line of sight.

Creature Ranks (Ch. 15): Creatures run from F to SSS; SS and SSS jump in power. Stats come from rank and size (Creature Stats by Rank).

Cover (Ch. 14): Draw a line from your square to the target's. Through the front and back of the square it hides behind: full cover, no shot. Only a side and the back, or a corner: half cover, -2 dice.

Armor Penetration (Ch. 12): Lowers the Armor part of a target's BDT for your hit only. Full Armor Penetration ignores all of it. Never lowers Fortitude (including Resilience).

Magic Penetration (Ch. 12): Lowers the magic part of a target's BDT the same way.

Upkeep Phase: Start of your turn: recover mana and AP, pay upkeep for persistent effects, and resolve Burning, Bleeding, Poisoned, and similar triggers.

Planning Phase: Declare movement, item use, attacks, and spells. The Storyteller declares enemy actions.

Move or Use Item Phases: Before and after your action: move your zones, use a belt item, or both by spending AP.

Action Phase: Roll your attack against the target's defense. If you win, your excess successes carry into the damage roll.

Damage Phase: Roll your damage pool, add your excess successes, and compare the total to the target's thresholds.

Ending Phase: Resolve end-of-turn triggers and conditions.

Combat Actions (Ch. 12): Instead of an attack. Contests against an adjacent target: Shove (pushed 1 zone), Trip (Prone), Grapple (Grappled), Disarm (weapon lands in an adjacent zone). Hide (Dexterity + Stealth beside cover). Total Defense (+2 defense dice until your next turn). Take Cover (+1 defense die beside cover). Aim (+2 dice on your next ranged attack at that target).

Damage and Health

ADT (Aggravated Damage Threshold) (Ch. 6): BDT x3. Meeting it deals 1 Aggravated.

Bashing, Lethal, Aggravated: The three tiers of damage, from bruises to deep wounds to severe trauma that can leave lasting marks.

BDT (Bashing Damage Threshold) (Ch. 6): Fortitude + Armor, plus any magic. Fortitude includes Resilience if you have that Merit. Meeting it deals 1 Bashing.

Bright and Shade Damage (Ch. 13): Bright damages undead but not the living; Shade damages the living but not undead. Both can help or hinder either.

Damage Pool (Ch. 14): A weapon's damage Attribute + its Rating.

Damage Subtypes: Slashing, Piercing, Kinetic, Burning, Electric, Constricting, Crushing, and Falling describe how an attack hurts and may interact with armor or abilities as the Storyteller rules. An attack never names Bashing, Lethal, or Aggravated; comparing its damage to the target's thresholds decides that.

Falling Damage: 1d10 per 10 feet (one vertical zone). The face values are the damage, not successes; below BDT means no damage.

Healing Tiers (Ch. 13): Healing spells give a PHP. Basic: neutralize 1 Bashing or slake 1 Lethal. Advanced: neutralize 2 Bashing, neutralize 1 Lethal, or slake 1 Aggravated. Expert: neutralize 4 Bashing, 2 Lethal, or 1 Aggravated. Master regrows lost limbs, outside combat only.

LDT (Lethal Damage Threshold) (Ch. 6): BDT x2. Meeting it deals 1 Lethal.

PHP (Pool of Healing Points) (Ch. 13): Healing points equal to the cast successes. The target spends them on their own turn at no action cost. Most pools end at the end of the target's next turn.

Slake and Neutralize (Ch. 13): To slake a damage box is to lower it one tier; to neutralize it is to remove it.

Wound Penalties: The -1, -2, and -4 marked on the health track, applied to rolls as damage fills it. Penalties are cumulative across damage types.

Conditions

Bleeding: Each Upkeep, roll Fortitude + Endurance against the effect's rating: success takes 1 Bashing, failure 1 Lethal. Lasts until healed or Cleansed. Applying pressure (no other actions) stops the next tick.

Blinded: Cannot cast any spell or make an attack that targets a person or zone.

Burning: Each Upkeep, roll Fortitude + Endurance against the effect's rating: success takes 1 Bashing, failure 1 Lethal. Ends with Water Magic, or by spending a full turn putting it out.

Chilled: Penalty dice on Dexterity rolls. Ends with Fire Magic or Cleanse.

Exhausted: No free move or free action; all movement and attacks cost AP. Lasts until an Upkeep Phase outside the affected zone.

Grappled: No belt items or attacks. During Upkeep, roll Strength + Athletics to break free; on a failure, the Action Phase can be used to try again.

Impairment: A lost or useless limb. An arm halves all dice pools (rounded up); a leg removes free movement, so all movement costs AP.

Mana Bleed (Ch. 13): Losing mana during an Upkeep Phase from an ongoing effect, mostly Shade.

Petrified: Works like Rooted and Grappled at once: no moving, attacking, casting, or items. During Upkeep, roll Strength + Athletics against the cast successes to break free.

Poisoned: Each Upkeep, take the poison's damage value against your thresholds. Roll Fortitude + Endurance each turn to lower it. Ends at 0 or with Cleanse.

Prone: Knocked down. Standing up counts as moving 1 zone.

Rooted: Cannot move zones.

Stunned (Ch. 12): Not a separate condition: a stun is written as losing AP on your next turn.

Incapacitation and Death

Incapacitation: Reached when all Aggravated boxes are full, or a hit lands in a column that is already full. The player chooses Fall Unconscious, Risk It, or Blaze of Glory.

Fall Unconscious: The character drops until at least one Aggravated is removed or lowered to Lethal.

Risk It: Keep fighting with all penalties. One more Aggravated and the character dies.

Blaze of Glory: One final turn: every die in the pool counts as a 10 for both attack and damage, and the opponent defends normally. Then the character dies, and cannot be resurrected.

Mana Countdown (Ch. 13): When a character dies without choosing to, the mana they held counts down 1 per day. At 0, an uncremated body rises as mindless undead.

Resurrection (Ch. 13): The six-school combination spell (Fire, Water, Air, Nature, Bright, Shade at 6 dots) that can return someone who died without choosing to, before their mana countdown runs out.

Dungeon Respawn (Ch. 4): In the Grand Dungeon a bracelet sends a fallen delver to their locker instead: all Lethal and Aggravated removed, every Bashing box marked.

Social Combat

Acting Against Your Scales (Ch. 7): Low positions can only cost Willpower; minor violations at a High position cost Willpower, severe ones cost Sanity.

Critical Comeback (Ch. 11): A defender who rolls 5 or more successes over the attacker ends the exchange in their own favor.

End of a Social Combat (Ch. 11): It ends when either side fills their Willpower row with X's, or when 5 or more damage lands in one retort.

Social Combat (Ch. 11): The attacker rolls a Social Attribute + an Ability; the defender rolls Willpower + Composure. Each excess success deals 1 Willpower damage.

Social Moves (Ch. 11): Persuade (Charisma + Persuasion), Intimidate (Charisma or Manipulation + Presence), Deceive (Manipulation + Subterfuge), Taunt (Manipulation + Expression; a win means they must attack you next turn).

Mana and Magic

Attuned (Ch. 13): An effect or item that holds part of your M/T for as long as it is active. Ending it returns the mana during the next Upkeep Phase.

Battle Aura (Ch. 13): The discipline of running mana through body and weapon. From Rank 4 its techniques can be paid with 1 to 4 Willpower, separate from the once-per-turn spend.

Committed Mana (Ch. 13): Mana paid as upkeep for a persistent effect; it counts toward your M/T.

Countdown (Ch. 13): A persistent effect lasts turns equal to half the mana spent, rounded up. Paying upkeep freezes the countdown for that turn.

Default Ability (Ch. 12, 13): The free ability that comes with every dot of a Combat Style, casting school, or Battle Aura.

Elemental Advantage (Ch. 13): Strong advantage +2 dice, moderate +1: Fire beats Nature and Earth, Nature beats Earth and Air, Earth beats Air and Water, Air beats Water and Fire, Water beats Fire and Nature.

Imbuing (Ch. 13): Carrying one casting school's energy on a physical attack, through Battle Aura from Rank 4 or through an Augmentation. Not combination magic.

Mana Burn (Ch. 11): Spending past your M/T: 1 over deals 1 Bashing, 2 over 1 Lethal, 3 over 1 Aggravated, 4 over 2 Aggravated, then 1 more Aggravated per mana. Any Aggravated from it leaves a permanent mark.

Mana Pool (MP) (Ch. 11): (Magic dots + Circle Magic dots + every Magical Knowledge dot) x Mana.

Mana Recovery (Ch. 11): Each Upkeep: 1 mana at 1 to 3 Mana dots, 2 at 4 to 6, 3 at 7 to 8, 4 at 9, 5 at 10, plus 1 if you took damage. Outside combat, a few minutes of rest refills the pool.

Mana Sickness (Ch. 11): Damage from mana density: Bashing in elevated areas, Lethal in extreme ones. Arrivals with no Mana take Bashing from the world itself.

Max Mana Per Turn (M/T) (Ch. 11): Fortitude + Mana: the most mana you can safely spend in one turn. Upkeep costs count toward it.

Prepared Spells (Ch. 13): You can have spells prepared equal to your Intelligence. Swap freely outside combat; the list is fixed during a fight.

Running Dry (Ch. 11): Spending your pool down to 0 knocks you unconscious until you recover mana.

Unchosen Option (Ch. 12, 13): The option or spell you did not pick at a rank, bought later for Rank x2 XP.

Upcast (Ch. 13): Extra mana beyond a spell's cost, declared before rolling, either to add dice or to augment the effect, never both.

Upkeep (Ch. 13): The cost to keep a persistent effect going each Upkeep Phase: always the spell's mana cost minus 1.

Spell Keywords

AoE Shapes: Cone (1, then 3, then 5 zones forward; can hit allies), Line (every zone in one direction), and squares of 2x2, 3x3, 4x4, or 5x5 zones.

Attack: Deals damage to a target.

Buff: Gives an ally a positive effect.

CC (Crowd Control): Restricts movement or takes away actions.

Cleanse: Removes conditions from the living, or applies them to undead.

Conjuration: Creates an entity that acts on its own turn after a command; it cannot act the turn it appears.

Corrupt: Removes conditions from undead, or applies them to the living.

Debuff: Puts penalty dice or reduced stats on an enemy.

Drain: Removes mana directly from a target's pool.

Healing: Gives a target a PHP.

Persistent: Has a duration and can be kept going with upkeep.

Reaction: A spell cast in response to something on another character's turn.

Utility: A non-damage effect that changes the situation.

Vitality Pool: Health for a conjured entity or effect. At 0, it is destroyed.

Combination and Circle Magic

Circle Magic (Ch. 13): Ritual magic made outside combat: scrolls, divination, summoning, teleportation, contracts, bindings, wards, augmentation, spatial magic, and time magic. A finished circle can be used at any time. Each discipline is its own Magical Knowledge with its own dots.

Combination Cap (Ch. 13): A combination works at the lowest dot rating among its schools.

Combination Spell (Ch. 13): A spell woven from two to six schools. It needs dots in every school equal to its rank and costs schools x dots in mana (4, 9, 16, 25, or 36).

Creation Magic (Ch. 13): The combination of all seven schools at 7 dots: the only magic that brings new autonomous life into the world. It can be the work of many casters, or of a town's belief, over time.

Learning Combinations (Ch. 13): Take one free in place of a rank's standard spell when a new dot meets its prerequisites, or buy it for Rank x2 XP per school.

Familiar (Ch. 9): A creature bonded by the Familiar Bond Merit. It works like a SumMonz, stays out in its basic form at no mana cost, and does not count toward your SumMonz on the field. Your Familiar Bond dots add to its Attack and Defense pools.

Item Circles (Ch. 13): Summoning circles drawn on items you own so you can call them later. The largest size you can call equals your Summoning Magic dots.

SumMonz (Ch. 13): A summoned fiend or soul summoned being kept on a card etched with its soul mark. You can have 1 on the field at 1 to 2 dots of Summoning Magic, 2 at 3 to 4, 3 at 5 to 6, and 4 at 7. Second, third, and fourth forms unlock at 3, 5, and 7 dots.

Weapons, Armor, and Items

Arcane Enhancement (Ch. 9): A Merit that boosts a trait with a built-in enhancement and permanently lowers Max Mana Per Turn.

Armor (Ch. 14): Only body armor and helmets add Armor. Light armor comes in ranks F to D (1 to 3), Medium D to B (3 to 5), Heavy B to S (5 to 7). Your Physical column limits what you can wear.

Artifact (Ch. 13, 14): An item that gained random magical abilities in a place of dense mana. Attuning holds M/T equal to its Rating.

Augmentation (Ch. 13, 14): A magical or mechanical enhancement built into the body or a weapon with an augment slot. Body augmentations hold M/T equal to their Rating.

Belt Slots (Ch. 14): 3 items set before combat. The first used in a turn is free, the second costs 1 AP, the third 2 more. Slots 4 to 7 open only when an item or ability grants them.

Implement (Ch. 13, 14): A magic item made on purpose for one known effect. Costs mana each use; no attunement.

Item Rating (Ch. 14): A rank from F to S (1 to 7). It adds to the rolls the item is made for.

Shield (Ch. 14): Adds its own Armor bonus: +1 Light, +2 Medium, +3 Heavy.

Weapon Rating (Ch. 14): Comes from rank alone: F +1 up to S +7. Added to the damage pool.
