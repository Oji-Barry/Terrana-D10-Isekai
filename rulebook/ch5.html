Introduction

Everyone in Terrana knows who is on top. Most of them want to be.

From “A Survey of the Known World” by Liris Kant, Third Chair of Natural Philosophy, Monsera University

Terrana loves a ranking. Taverns argue over them, whole channels on the CCN are devoted to them, and children in Monsera can recite the Seven Strongest before they can recite their letters. I have recorded the three that matter most, with one warning: the bottom of every list changes faster than any book can be printed.

Three leaderboards define the peak of power and achievement in Terrana. The Seven Strongest ranks the most powerful beings in the world. The Slime King's Elite Four ranks the strongest warriors of Monsera. The Deep Delver Leaderboard ranks the parties who have gone deepest into the Grand Dungeon. Together they are the goals that drive many ambitious adventurers.

The Seven Strongest

How the Titles Work

The Seven Strongest is a world-recognized ranking with one strict rule: a God Rank title can only be won by defeating its current holder in combat. The defeated holder drops to Imperial Rank, and the victor takes the title.

Because each title carries its rank and passes from holder to holder, the ranking lists titles rather than names.

1. The Dragon God

Profile: The son of the first Dragon God, he has walked Terrana since before recorded history. He travels the world alone, keeping an eternal watch against any chance of Hitogami's return.

2. The Slime King

Holder: Julius Geoffrey Golbjorn, ruler of Monsera.

Profile: His power is so far beyond anyone else's that the ranking barely measures it (see the Storyteller Note in Chapter 3). He spends most of his time in the royal suite of Monsera Palace playing video games and ignores the ranking completely.

3. The Fighting God

Holder: Badigadi, the Wise Demon King of Biegoya.

Profile: An Immortal Demon King with six arms and the devoted husband of Empress Kishirika. He is famous for his love of lively parties and good company.

4. The Death God

Holder: Classified.

Profile: The Death God's true identity is known only to the king of the Dragon King Kingdom and his highest-ranking generals. The holder is gaunt and wears distinctive, easily recognized armor. Ironically, the Death God hates killing, despite being exceptionally good at it.

5 to 7. The Martial Gods

Titles: The Sword God, the North God, and the Water God.

Profile: Unlike the top four, these titles are always changing hands. They pass back and forth through formal, high-stakes duels and challenge matches across the continents. Monsera University's own Professor Aldric Thorne currently holds the title of Water God.

The Slime King's Elite Four

The Elite Four are the Slime King's personal vanguard. Julius Geoffrey Golbjorn never wanted a personal guard, but during Monsera's early years its monster peoples saw a circle of champion warriors as a great source of pride. Today the Elite Four are the four strongest active citizens of the island nation.

The Trial of Ascension

The Festival Tournament: Every year, during the two-week festival celebrating Monsera's founding, the citizens host a huge martial tournament in the Grand Arena.

The Gauntlet: The tournament's champion earns the right to challenge the Elite Four. The challenger faces one member at a time over four months, with weeks of rest and training between each duel.

Rank Displacement: A challenger who defeats a member of the Elite Four takes that member's rank, and every member below it moves down one place. Anyone pushed out of the Elite Four must win next year's tournament to earn a chance at returning.

The Sovereign Right: A challenger who defeats all four members earns the right to challenge the Slime King himself for the throne. The same right goes automatically to anyone who clears all one hundred floors of the Grand Dungeon.

Historical Record: Only one challenger has ever defeated the entire Elite Four and faced the King. She lost to the Slime King, but her effort earned her the top place in the Elite Four, which she has held ever since.

The Elite Four

1. Immortal Devil Queen Sciel

Heritage: Devil.

Profile: The founder of Sciel City, better known as Nite City, and the only challenger ever to sweep the Elite Four. She earned her place in a legendary fight against the Slime King and has held the top rank ever since.

2. General Vahn Grimjaw

Heritage: Elder Orc.

Profile: A seasoned military veteran and a central pillar of Monsera's internal defense forces.

3. Korac

Heritage: Armored Minotaur.

Profile: A powerful warrior who first rose through the ranks of the Laborers Guild.

4. Mirage

Heritage: Arachne.

Profile: The newest member of the Elite Four, who won her place by taking the most recent annual tournament.

The Deep Delver Leaderboard

The Deep Delver Leaderboard is a community board on a site on the CCN. It tracks the seven parties who have gone deepest into the Grand Dungeon, and it is updated both by people who work at the Arena and by the wider community. No party has ever cleared the final ten floors, so parties are ranked by the deepest floor they have reached.

Only part of the board is verified. A party checks in with the guild each time it reaches a floor ending in five, and those check-ins are the only official record. Anything deeper is known only through the party's own word or the community's. The only reason anyone knows the Ashen Vanguard reached Floor 93 is that they live streamed it.
