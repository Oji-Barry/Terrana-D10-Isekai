Introduction

Five continents, two island realms, and one sea that ties them together.

From “A Survey of the Known World” by Liris Kant, Third Chair of Natural Philosophy, Monsera University

Most people in Terrana never travel far from where they were born, and many hold firm opinions about places they have never seen. This survey describes the lands as my colleagues and I have found them, from the frozen north of the Central Continent to the bogs of the Demon Continent.

Borders move and rulers change. Where something here is out of date by the time you read it, I ask for the reader's patience. The coastlines, at least, have held still for a long time.

Terrana is made up of seven major landmasses around the Ringus Sea: the Central, Demon, Milis, Begaritt, and Heaven Continents, the island nation of Monsera, and the Shaded Isles. The Merfolk and Fishfolk hold the waters between them.

The Central Continent

The largest landmass in Terrana and home to the greatest share of its Hyuman population. The Red Dragon Mountain Range splits the continent into three regions: the North, the West, and the South. The Dragon King Mountain Range runs down its southeastern side, with dense forest along its slopes.

The North

A frozen, unforgiving expanse where survival depends on willpower and magical innovation.

The Three Magic Kingdoms: Ranoa, Neris, and Basherant were historically centers of traditional magical learning in a cold and difficult region. Today they are modernizing quickly, using mana-powered heating networks and agricultural magic imported from Monsera to tame their climate. Their partnership with Monsera University is one of the most productive academic relationships in the world.

The Kingdom of Biheiril: Set among the northeastern forests and mountains, this Hyuman kingdom keeps long-standing ties with the Ogres of the nearby islands. Its deepest woods shelter the continent's only Superd settlement, a fierce and insular people who protect the realm from threats beyond Hyuman capability while the kingdom leaves them to live as they choose.

The Holy Land of the Sword: A land of permanent winter in the far northwest, devoted entirely to mastering the Silver Arc Style of long blades. Many who hope to win the title of Sword God come here to train. It remains stubborn, deeply traditional, and largely unchanged by time.

The Ogre Islands: A chain of islands off the northeastern coast and the ancestral home of the island Ogres. Most of its people train to master the Ten Count Style of boxing or the Drifting Leaf Style of kung fu.

The West: The Asura Kingdom

The most fertile and prosperous region on the continent, dominated by the Asura Kingdom, the most powerful Hyuman nation in the world.

A Modern Kingdom: Once an absolute monarchy, Asura is now a constitutional monarchy governed by a parliament. The royal family functions mainly as high-society celebrities and ceremonial figureheads. Merit-based inheritance rules mean titles do not automatically pass to the eldest child.

The Capital City of Ars: The political and economic heart of the nation, governed as its own district apart from the four regions. Ars is the largest and most populous city in the Hyuman world and the seat of both the royal family and parliament.

Outside the capital, the kingdom is divided into four regions, each traditionally overseen by a branch of the Greyrat noble line:

The Fittoa Region (Northeast): Ruled by the Boreas Greyrat family. An agricultural and pastoral frontier anchored by its administrative hub and fortress city, Roa.

The Milbots Region (East): Governed by the Notos Greyrat family. It sits along key routes and serves as the kingdom's main gateway for continental trade.

The Donati Region (Northwest): Overseen by the Zephyrus Greyrat family. A strategic and agricultural anchor for the northern borders.

The Wisiru Region (South): Governed by the Euros Greyrat family. The southern territory is known for its distinct climate, geography, and natural resources.

The South

A fractured territory defined by a stark divide between lawless chaos and corporate rule.

The Strife Zone: The northern half of the region is an ungoverned wilderness of tribal towns caught in permanent, low-level warfare. Void Openings breach here more often than anywhere else on the continent. It has become a haven for insular, low-tech refugee villages escaping the corporate regimes to the south. Monsera has recently made strides in outreach by opening small schools for any child born in the zone.

Breakwater: On the coast just east of the Strife Zone sits Breakwater, Monsera's military port and trade gateway on the continent. It is described in full in Chapter 3.

The Corporate Kingdoms: Shirone (Rone Co.), Kikka (Kik Co.), and Sanika (Ani Co.) keep the appearance of crowns and noble titles, but real legislative power belongs to corporate CEOs and boards of directors. Society is divided by literal walls into three tiers: Shareholders, Managers, and Laborers, with perimeter walls built to keep workers from escaping. Decades of suppressed revolutions have left their working districts in ruins.

The Dragon King Kingdom: At the southern tip of the continent, this proud monarchy is the trade bridge between Monsera's southern operations and the Begaritt Continent, with East Port and West Port handling most of that traffic. It is the third most powerful nation in the world and has modernized modestly while keeping its cultural identity.

The Demon Continent

A harsh land east of the Ringus Sea. Its ambient mana density is higher than anywhere else in Terrana, which makes for deadly terrain and some of the most dangerous monsters in the world. The continent is divided into ten domains, each ruled by an Immortal Demon King, and all of them honor the title of the Empress.

Most trade with the rest of the world passes through Kurasuma in the northwest and through Wind Port and Zanto Port in the south. Migurd Village, home of the Migurd, and the city of Rikarisu both sit in the northeast.

Empress Kishirika Kishirisu

The chaotic, current Empress of the Demon World, brought to this realm by Necross Lacross during the God Wars. She is volatile, but legendary for her generosity, and has been known to gift one of her coveted Magic Eyes to those who help her. The Magic Eyes and how a character can earn one appear in Chapter 9.

The Ten Domains

Badigadi, Biegoya: The husband of Kishirika Kishirisu, known across realms as the Fighting God and the Wise Demon King. His domain of Biegoya lies in the far northeast. Badigadi is one of the few beings the Slime King treats as a peer.

Beethove Tovetha, the Library Labyrinth: A monstrous black slime-octopus that rules the Library Labyrinth in the north, using its own ink and tentacles to record every piece of written lore in the world.

Raja Iblis Baglahagla: The pig-like King of the Orcs, whose domain covers the northwest of the continent.

Vita, the Abyssal King: A parasitic blue slime-demon ruling a domain in the west. Anyone who ingests Vita suffers blinding illusions and visions while Vita takes complete control of their mind and body.

Necross Lacross: A renowned tactician of several ancient conflicts who fought in the God Wars, and father to Atoferatofe and Badigadi. His domain lies at the heart of the continent.

Atoferatofe Ryback, Gaslow: A terrifying combatant and sister to Badigadi. She is infamous for kidnapping foreign royalty and challenging powerful warriors; those she defeats are conscripted as her “disciples.” Her domain, Gaslow, is a dark bog in the east, and a dark dragon is often seen flying over it.

Lynebyne, the Stone King: Ruler of a domain in the southwest of the continent.

Samedynomedy, Demon King of Light: Ruler of a domain in the south.

Patorsetor, the Enchantress Demon King: Ruler of a domain on the southeastern coast.

Qeblaqabla, the Evil Demon King: A floating orb leaking black liquid, ruling a domain at the continent's southern end.

Legends of the Demon Continent

The First Demon God: The multi-armed, six-horned progenitor of many immortal demons, slain during the God Wars.

Kirisis Calisis: The First Empress.

Malta: The Ogre God.

Laplace: The Demon God, born of demon and dragon blood.

The Milis Continent

Southeast of Monsera, the Milis Continent is split in two by the jagged Blue Dragon Mountain Range.

The Great Forest: The northern expanse and the ancestral home of the Beastfolk, whose territory covers most of the forest. The Holy Sword Highway runs through it, connecting the north of the continent to the south.

Elf Territory: In the forest's eastern reaches, the reclusive Elves live in balance between nature and some of the most advanced technology Monsera has produced.

Halfling Territory: On the hilly western coast, the Halflings have adopted small amounts of technology while staying close to their traditional roots.

Dwarf Territory: Along the northern side of the Blue Dragon Mountains, the Dwarves have taken a great interest in steam power. The technology started in Monsera, but the Dwarves have embraced it more than any other recent advancement.

The Holy Milis Kingdom: The southern expanse, with its capital at Milishion, and the seat of the Church of Milis. It is the second most powerful nation in Terrana and a genuinely integrated society, with Hyumans and Beastfolk each making up roughly half the population. The Holy Church of Milis, the royal families, and parliament form three co-equal branches of government, though historically the Church has had the greatest say.

The Begaritt Continent

A lawless, treacherous landmass in the southwest with no unified government. It is populated almost entirely by adventurers, outlaws, and those who wish to live outside the reach of any state. The continent is defined by its dangerous concentration of shifting Labyrinths and volatile magical anomalies.

Labyrinth Town Rapan: On the southern coast, the town of Rapan is built within the ribs of an enormous Behemoth skeleton and serves as the main base for those who delve the continent's Labyrinths.

The Heaven Continent

A sheer plateau resting three thousand meters above sea level in the north. It is home to the Skyfolk, winged, elf-like people whose aloof and detached outlook often makes them seem alien to outsiders.

The Hell Dungeon: Beneath the plateau lies the Hell Dungeon, a rift descending through nine layers. It is described in full in the section that follows.

The Hell Dungeon

The Hell Dungeon is a nine-layered rift beneath the Heaven Continent. Each layer is its own kingdom, ruled by a devil prince or queen, and together they make up what most of Terrana simply calls Hell. It is not to be confused with the Grand Dungeon of Monsera, though the naming has caused confusion before.

An Economy of Suffering

For as long as anyone can remember, Hell has run on suffering. Devils harvest mana from pain, fear, and despair, and the old powers of Hell built their kingdoms on that harvest. About sixty years ago, Sciel, Queen of the third layer, proved that devils can harvest and regenerate mana just as well from happiness and other positive emotions. She left Hell before the fighting truly began and, with the Slime King's help, founded Nite City in the Grand Dungeon, which has been a refuge for fleeing devils ever since.

Mahk, whose true name is Belethore, once ruled the second layer. He left as well, and today he runs the CCN's studios hidden in the Grand Dungeon. Their departures shook Hell to its foundations.

The War for the Old Ways

Zagan, Prince of the fourth layer, saw the new way of living as a threat to everything Hell was built on. He launched a war of conquest to unite Hell under his rule and preserve the old ways of suffering. His forces took the fifth layer and captured its queen, Gremory, and the sixth layer's ruler, Marchosias, now serves under him.

The second and third layers, left without their rulers, are now governed by Mahk's cousin, also named Belethore. He rules as Zagan's puppet, and with both layers under Zagan's control in all but name, the fighting has come to a standstill. Total chaos has been avoided in recent times through the diplomatic efforts of Monsera's Slime King.

Not every layer has fallen in line. Phenex of the seventh layer, Eligor of the eighth, and Astaroth of the ninth rule independently, and the three work together to keep Zagan's forces from conquering any further up into Hell. The seventh layer, bordering Marchosias's domain, is almost always in a constant state of battle. Lucifer of the first layer also remains independent.

The calm is not peace. Zagan has turned his attention to the world outside Hell, and his agents work to cause mayhem across Terrana.

Monsera

The island nation of Monsera sits in the southern Ringus Sea, between the Central Continent and the Milis Continent. It is the technological and magical center of modern Terrana. Its history and institutions are covered in full in Chapter 3.

The Capital: A sprawling metropolis governed by a guild-based parliamentary democracy and home to the world-renowned Monsera University.

The Grand Dungeon and Arena: Just east of the capital. The grand colosseum above is owned by the citizens of Monsera, while the 100-floor Dungeon below is co-owned by the Slime King and the Earth God. No adventuring party has ever cleared the final ten floors. The Dungeon is covered in Chapter 4.

Hero Rangers HQ: On the island's north shore stands the headquarters of the Hero Rangers Corps, who guard the world against the Rift.

The Shaded Isles

A mist-shrouded archipelago east of Monsera. The isles are part of the Monsera nation, much as an outlying island belongs to its mainland, but they are home to three ancient, sovereign noble houses of the Undead.

The Vampiric Kingdom: The largest of the isles, ruled by an ancient house of Vampires.

The Dullahan Kingdom: Southeast of the Vampiric Kingdom, ruled by a noble house of Dullahans.

The Liches' Domain: Just south of the Vampiric Kingdom, ruled by a noble house of Liches.

The Ringus Sea and Surrounding Waters

The stormy central ocean that connects every land in Terrana, and the open waters beyond its coasts.

The Rift: A permanent, catastrophic tear into the Void at the center of the sea. About once a year it ruptures and releases a Kaiju-class threat. These disasters are held back by the Hero Rangers Corps alone, operating from their stronghold on Monsera's north shore.

The Seafolk

The waters of Terrana belong to the Merfolk and the Fishfolk. Merfolk prefer the central waters of the Ringus Sea, while Fishfolk prefer the northern and southern waters. The two peoples intermingle and intermarry freely, but each tribe answers to its own sovereign Chieftain.
