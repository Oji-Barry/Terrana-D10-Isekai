Welcome to Terrana

Jimothy's D10 Isekai JRPG is a tabletop roleplaying game played with ten-sided dice. One player is the Storyteller, who describes the world, plays everyone the characters meet, and keeps the story moving. Everyone else plays a character of their own making. Together you decide what your characters attempt, and the dice decide how well it goes.

The Slime King arrived in Terrana about 150 years ago. In that time the world has advanced in technology, science, and magic until it holds many of the comforts of the 20th and 21st centuries of our own world. Where our world struggles with fuel and power, Terrana uses magic in its place.

New to the Game?

For a first session, a player only needs Chapter 6 and the chapters it points to. Keep a character sheet beside you and fill it in as you go. Everything else can be read as it comes up at the table.
