A Note on the World

Terrana Is Old

It is older than its current name, older than any of its kingdoms, older than the gods who shaped it and the war that nearly unmade it. What stands today was built by survivors: people who came from worlds that no longer exist and decided, collectively and not always politely, to make something that would last.

It is a world of dungeons and dragons, of pop stars and politicians, of ancient immortal demon kings and university students who are perpetually almost late to class.

"The Slime King understood from the beginning that Monsera's value would come from being accessible to everyone and beholden to no one."

Jimothy's D10 Isekai · Chapter 3
